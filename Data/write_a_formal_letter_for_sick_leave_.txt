Here is a formal letter for sick leave:

[Your Name]
[Your Address]
[City, State, Pincode]
[Date]

[Recipient's Name]
[Recipient's Designation]
[Company/Organization Name]
[Company/Organization Address]
[City, State, Pincode]

Dear [Recipient's Name],

I am writing to request a leave of absence from work due to health reasons. Unfortunately, I am currently unwell and am unable to attend work.

I am suffering from [briefly mention your illness or health issue, e.g. fever, flu, etc.]. My doctor has advised me to take [number of days] days of rest to recover fully. I am expecting to be back in the office on [expected date of return].

I apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and will make sure to catch up on any missed work as soon as I return. I will keep you updated on my status and will provide a medical certificate if required.

Please let me know if there are any pressing matters that need my attention in the meantime. I appreciate your understanding and support during this time.

Sincerely,

[Your Signature]
[Your Name]

Please note that you should customize this letter as per your needs and requirements. Also, make sure to keep a polite and professional tone throughout the letter.
